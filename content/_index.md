## The Story Behind Beri's Grill

*Beri's Grill started with family, grew among friends, and found its purpose in the community.*

For Chef Ron, cooking was always about bringing people together. It began
around the family grill, then grew into cooking for friends after soccer
games. Before long, those simple moments became backyard cookouts and
community gatherings filled with good food, laughter, music, and
unforgettable memories.

As the circle grew and people kept coming back for more, a dream was born —
to turn a passion for grilling and bringing people together into something
bigger. That dream became Beri's Grill.

The name carries the heart of our story. Chef Ron comes from the Nso
community of Cameroon in Central Africa, where "Beri" means "Thank You." It's
our way of saying thank you to the family who planted the roots, the friends
who encouraged the journey, the community that embraced us, and every
customer who supports the dream.

Today, Beri's Grill shares bold African flavors — from our signature Suya to
shawarma, fish, ribs, and chicken — while keeping the same spirit that
started it all: family, friendship, community, and gratitude.

**From family, to friends, to community. Served with gratitude.**
